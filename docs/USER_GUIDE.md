# User Guide & Rulebook: Classic Board Games

Welcome to **Classic Board Games**, a suite of timeless strategy games playable directly in your browser or on your Android mobile device.

---

## 🕹 Getting Started: Game Selection Menu

When launching the application, you are presented with the **Game Selection Menu**:
1. Choose from the available games (e.g. **Nine Men's Morris**, **English Checkers**, **Connect Four**, **Reversi**).
2. Click or tap **Play** to launch your match.
3. You can return to the Game Selection Menu at any time by clicking the **Games Menu** button in the top navigation bar.

## 📜 Game Rules & Guides

### 1. Nine Men's Morris (*Mühlespiel*)

#### The Board & Pieces
- Played on 24 intersection points arranged across 3 concentric squares.
- Each player starts with **9 pieces** (White plays first).

#### Game Phases
- **Phase 1 (Placement):** Players alternate placing one piece onto any vacant point.
- **Phase 2 (Movement):** Once all 18 pieces are placed, players alternate sliding a piece along a marked line to an adjacent vacant point.
- **Phase 3 (Flying / Springen):** When a player is reduced to exactly **3 pieces**, their pieces may jump to any vacant point on the board.

#### Mills & Captures
- Forming 3 pieces in a row along a marked line forms a **mill**.
- Closing a mill allows you to capture and remove one opponent piece.
- **Mill Protection Rule:** A piece inside an active mill cannot be captured unless the opponent has no non-mill pieces remaining.

#### Win & Draw Conditions
- **Victory:** Reduce the opponent to fewer than 3 pieces, or trap all opponent pieces so they have no legal moves.
- **Draw:** 3-fold position repetition or 50 consecutive moves without a capture.

---

### 2. English Checkers (*Draughts*) — *Coming in Milestone 2*
- Played on an 8x8 checkerboard using the 32 dark squares.
- 12 pieces per player. Normal pieces move diagonally forward one step.
- Jumps over opponent pieces are **mandatory**. Multi-jumps must be completed in the same turn.
- Reaching the opponent's back rank crowns a piece as a **King** (capable of moving and jumping backwards).

## ⚙ Controls & Settings

- **Difficulty Selector:** Choose between Level 1 (Beginner) through Level 5 (Master).
- **Player Color:** Play as White (First) or Black (Second). When choosing Black, the engine automatically calculates the opening move.
- **Undo:** Step back moves if you make an accidental misclick.
- **New Game:** Reset the current board to a clean opening position.
